ABOUT PROF. DR. MELİHA TAN

Professional biography

Prof. Dr. Meliha Tan completed her medical education at Çukurova University, specialized in neurology at Cumhuriyet University and earned a PhD in physiology at Atatürk University.

She held academic appointments at Karadeniz Technical University and Başkent University, serving as a professor of neurology at Başkent University from 2010 to 2015.
